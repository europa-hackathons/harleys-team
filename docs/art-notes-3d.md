# Rooftop rescue in 3D

Build for the view behind and slightly above the hero. From there, her bun, streaming cape and forward glove should each have a clear silhouette. Use matte, flat-shaded materials, spheres with about eight segments around, and cylinders with six to eight sides. [Chosen concept](../assets/concepts/concept-3d.png)

## Flying hero

Model her standing first. One unit runs from boot sole to crown, with the bun above it. Y points up. Widths below run shoulder to shoulder.

| Part | Primitives and proportions | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Suit | Two tapered cylinders form the hips and torso from Y 0.45 to 0.75. Widen from a 0.19 waist to 0.29 across the shoulders. | Teal `#1FA39A` |
| Head and hair | A 0.25-high sphere sits on the shoulders. Overlap its back and crown with a flattened hair sphere. Set a 0.14-wide bun high on the back so it breaks the outline. | Skin `#E7A37F`, hair `#593426` |
| Goggles | Wrap a thin cylinder band around eye level. Place two shallow yellow boxes on the face, each with a smaller teal lens. Keep the band visible from the side. | Frame `#FFD45C`, lenses `#1FA39A` |
| Arms and gloves | Give each arm two 0.14-long cylinders joined at the elbow. Add a wide cuff, a 0.09-long sphere palm and a small sphere thumb. | Suit teal, gloves `#B57EDC` |
| Legs and boots | Give each leg a 0.24-long tapered thigh and a 0.21-long lower leg. Colour the lowest 0.15 lavender and overlap the sole with a flattened sphere for the toe. | Suit teal, boots `#B57EDC`, boot bands `#FFD45C` |
| Belt | Fit a 0.035-high cylinder around the waist. | Gold `#FFD45C` |
| Cape | Fan three thin box panels from the shoulder blades. Make each about 0.15 wide and 0.42 long. Overlap them at the collar and tilt the outer hems to different heights for a folded silhouette. | Coral `#F2635F` |

For flight, pitch the body forward about 65 degrees from upright. Reach one glove past the head, trail the other beside the hip and bend one knee. Lift the cape clear of the belt and sweep it back toward the boots. The web starts just beyond the forward palm in cyan `#4FE3E0`.

## Townsperson

Make a 0.78-unit figure with a 0.22-high sphere head, box torso, cylinder limbs and sphere hands. Use a warm grey shirt (`#C9B8B0`) and two brown vest panels (`#795041`) with a strip of shirt visible between them. Flatten a brown sphere into a cap and slide a thin box brim forward over two dark dot eyes.

While waiting, lean the torso toward the hero, rest one hand on the chimney lip and wave the other above the cap. Pivot the forearm at the elbow so the hand crosses clear sky. On rescue, turn toward the hero, throw both arms into a wide V and give the whole figure two short hops. Three gold sparkles above the cap carry the cheer across the rooftops.

## House and chimney

Start with a warm grey `#C9B8B0` box about 2 units wide, 1.6 deep and 1.4 high. Make a triangular gable from a three-sided cylinder with its axis along the ridge and one vertex up. Stretch it to span the walls. Lay two thin roof-red `#B8405E` boxes along the slopes and extend them 0.10 past the eaves.

Set shallow glowing `#FFD45C` window boxes just proud of the facade. Cross each with two thin brown bars for four bright panes. Offset a square warm grey chimney toward one end of the ridge. Give the rescue chimney a 0.65-wide shaft, a wider box lip and a dark inset square on top. Place the waiting figure behind the front lip with its vest and waving arm visible.

## Street props

- **Streetlamp.** Stack a square foot, narrow charcoal cylinder post, yellow emissive box lantern and four-sided cone lid. A height of 1.2 units puts the glow above a townsperson's head.
- **Round tree.** Sink a short brown cylinder trunk into a faceted green sphere (`#667B51`) about 0.8 units across. Vary canopy size along the street.
- **Crate.** Start with a 0.35-unit brown box. Edge its front with lighter brown strips and cross two thin diagonal boxes inside the frame.

## Night sky and light

Use sky purple `#4B2A8C` overhead. Scatter tiny pale stars sparsely. For a featured gold star, surround a flattened sphere with five short outward-facing cones in `#FFD45C`. Keep the brightest stars clear of waving hands.

Use a soft hemisphere light with lavender sky `#D4C5F1` and muted plum ground `#735274`. Add a warm directional light (`#FFE0AF`) from above and one side to reveal the facets. Give windows and lanterns a little yellow emission. Blend distant roofs into violet fog (`#70509A`), starting beyond the active rescue chimney so its cap and hand stay distinct.
