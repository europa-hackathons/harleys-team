# Game plan

_Living English game plan, written from the Team's Design answers and kept up to date as wishes change the game. English only; no names or personal details. The Helper's name (chosen by the Team) is **Dot**._

## The game
- **What is it about?** A candy land chase: a world made of sweets (lollipop trees, gumdrop hills, chocolate rivers, sprinkles).
- **What does the player do?** Races their unicorn along a rainbow/candy track against a rival unicorn, collecting candy on the way.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys. Left/Right to steer between lanes, Up to jump.
- **Score / win / lose:** Most candy wins. Both unicorns collect candy during the race; when the race ends, whoever has more candy wins.
- **What makes it fun?** Rainbow speed boost: grab a rainbow pickup and ZOOM forward for a few seconds.
- **Round length:** aim for 60–90 seconds of play (a 60-second race is the first target).

## Look and feel
- **Style:** 2D cartoon, side view, like concept picture 1: bright candy colours (pinks, mint, lemon, sky blue), rainbow track, lollipop trees, frosted gumdrop hills, a chocolate river under a candy-cane bridge. Player unicorn: cream with a pink mane and heart marks. Rival: mint green with a star mark. Both have golden horns.
- **Concept pictures:** `assets/concepts/unicorn-race-1.png` (chosen look), `assets/concepts/unicorn-race-2.png` (3D toy look, not chosen).

## Wish list
- Starry sky: a twinkling night sky full of stars behind candy land (replaces the daytime sky of concept 1; candy colours stay bright).
- Super awesome rainbow ("super bra"): the rainbow track should be extra bright, glowing and sparkly, the star of the show, especially against the night sky.
