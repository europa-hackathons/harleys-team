# Game plan

_The living English game plan. The Helper keeps it up to date as wishes change the game. English only; no names or personal details._

## The game
- **What is it about?** An original flying superhero woman (goggles, cape, laser power, spider-style climbing) who patrols the rooftops of a cozy town at night and climbs up chimneys. She is our own character, not any existing hero.
- **What does the player do?** Rescue townsfolk stuck on rooftops and chimneys, who wave for help.
- **Controls:** Arrow keys to fly/move. Space bar shoots a web line to swing (from chimney to chimney).
- **Score / win / lose:** Every person rescued counts ("RESCUED: N"). You win by rescuing people; exact target (e.g. rescue N before the timer ends) to be decided during the first prototype.
- **What makes it fun?** The rescued people thank you a LOT: big happy reactions, cheers, hearts/stars, thank-you bubbles.
- **Round length:** aim for 60–90 seconds of play.

## Style
- **2D or 3D / camera view:** 3D (three.js), third-person camera behind and a little above the hero, looking out over the rooftops.
- **Palette (hex):**
  - Night sky purple `#4B2A8C`
  - Hero suit teal `#1FA39A`
  - Cape coral `#F2635F`
  - Gloves/boots lavender `#B57EDC`
  - Window glow / stars / web yellow-cyan accents `#FFD45C` (web line `#4FE3E0`)
  - Roof red `#B8405E` (chimneys/walls warm grey `#C9B8B0`)
- **Shapes and outlines:** low-poly toy look built from simple primitives (boxes, spheres, cylinders, cones); chunky rounded proportions; flat or simple shaded colours; soft lighting; no outlines, no detailed textures. Warm glowing windows, a few streetlamps and round trees.
- **Chosen concept picture:** `assets/concepts/concept-3d.png`

## Features
_One short entry per feature, written clearly enough that a building Friend can build it: what it does, how it looks, what happens at the edges (crashes, falling off, running out of time). Grows during question time._

- **Fly up and down:** the Team loves flying and wants to go higher. Arrows still steer as now; **W** flies up and **S** flies down. She can go from just above the rooftops up to well above the town (high enough to see far, not so high the townsfolk are lost; suggested range about roof height + 2 up to ~3x the current cruise height). The camera follows smoothly. Space web-zips still work from any height.
- **Land and walk:** hold S at the lowest height to land. On the ground she walks around (arrows), can take off again (W), and can shoot her web from the ground to zip straight up to a chimney (Space), like a spider swinging up a wall.
- **People pop out of chimneys:** like in the concept picture, townsfolk stand INSIDE a chimney, visible from the waist up with their arms over the rim, waving for help. A new person appears by popping up out of a chimney (a springy jack-in-the-box pop with a little puff of sparkles/soot-free smoke), and when rescued they pop out and cheer before vanishing in sparkles.
- **Clouds:** puffy low-poly clouds (clusters of flattened spheres) drifting slowly above the town, tinted by the sky: pink-orange at sunset and sunrise, soft lavender-grey at night. She can fly right through them: when she flies into a cloud, a rainbow bursts out of it (a colourful arc of the palette colours plus rainbow stripes that pops out and fades after a couple of seconds, with a little sparkle).
- **Sunrise party (end of round):** when the sun comes up, everyone she rescued this round appears on the rooftops cheering and waving, with confetti and hearts, before the end screen (see assets/concepts/idea-4.png). The Team decided: **everyone cheers and fireworks go off** (colourful low-poly firework bursts in palette colours over the town, a few seconds of show, then the end screen).
- **Pets and kites:** besides people, sometimes a cute cat waits on a chimney or a kite is tangled on one; rescuing them counts too (see assets/concepts/idea-1.png).
- **Shooting stars:** now and then a shooting star streaks across the night sky; fly up and touch it for +5 bonus (see assets/concepts/idea-2.png).
- **Traffic on the streets:** (the Team wanted a racing game; the Grown-up kept the rescue game and offered traffic instead.) Chunky low-poly toy cars in palette colours drive along the streets between the houses, turning at corners, with glowing headlights and tail lights at night (off by day, like the windows). They are scenery: no crashing, the hero simply passes over or beside them when walking. The Team wants **a bus and cars**: one long chunky town bus plus a few small cars.
- **Shadows from the sun and moon:** the main light follows the sun at sunset/sunrise and the moon at night, and casts soft shadows: houses, chimneys, trees, the hero, townsfolk and vehicles cast shadows on roofs and streets. Moon shadows are fainter and bluish; sun shadows are long and warm at sunset/sunrise. Keep it smooth (one shadow-casting light, shadow area following the hero).
- **Background scenery (like the concept):** beyond the town: rolling green low-poly hills with pine trees, a river with water that catches the light, an arched stone bridge over it, and a little church/castle with towers on top of a hill with glowing windows; plus a few different buildings in the distance (towers, a mill, bigger houses). Built from simple shapes, scenery only.
- **House variety:** houses in the style of assets/concepts/house-model-2.png (tall lavender chimneys with brick knobs), mixed with a two-storey version (house-model-3.png) and a porch version (house-model-4.png).
- **One night, sunset to sunrise (the round timer):** she is a night-time hero. A round lasts **60 seconds** (was 90) and is one whole night. It starts at sunset: the sun is going down behind the mountains and the sky is orange-pink. It quickly fades to the night purple with stars for most of the round. Near the end it brightens to a pink-orange dawn, and the sun comes up when time is up. Goal: rescue as many people as possible before sunrise. There is no fixed win number; the end screen celebrates the count ("The sun is up! You rescued N people!").

## Built so far
- **First playable version, "Rooftop Rescue"** (working title; the Team may rename it).
  - Title screen with the game name, a short "How to play" line and a PLAY button; the town slowly spins behind it.
  - 3D night town built from simple shapes, made to look more like the concept picture: about 100 cozy houses with warm grey and cream walls, wall-coloured gables and red roof slabs that stick out past the walls; some houses show their gable (with the door) to the street, some their long side. Big golden windows with a frame cross (four panes) and a sill, a little attic window in tall gables. Chunky chimneys with a wider rim and a dark opening. Streetlamps are box lanterns with a dark pointed cap and a square foot, with a soft amber pool of light on the ground at night; some have a small round tree and a wooden crate beside them. Faceted round green trees. Two staggered rows of low, broad mountains with tiny houses at their feet, lots of starry sky above. Lighting: a soft lavender glow from the sky plus a warm light from one side, so red roofs and the coral cape keep their colour at night; far-away roofs fade into violet mist.
  - The hero (teal suit, coral cape, lavender gloves/boots, yellow goggles, brown hair bun) flies above the rooftops; the camera follows from behind and above.
  - Controls: hold ⬆️ to fly forward, ⬇️ to slow down/back up, ⬅️ ➡️ to turn. SPACE shoots a cyan web line to a chimney in front and zips her there (it picks chimneys with people waiting first).
  - **Fly up and down:** W flies up, S flies down (smooth speed-up, she tilts nose-up/nose-down). She can go from 2 above the roofs and chimneys (she glides up over houses, never through them) up to 3x the old cruise height. With no key held she keeps her height, also after a web zip. Higher up the camera pulls back and looks down more. The title screen "How to play" mentions W/S.
  - 7 townsfolk are stuck INSIDE chimneys: they stand in the chimney, seen from the waist up, one arm over the rim and the other waving, with a "HELP!" bubble. Each one appears with a springy jack-in-the-box pop out of the chimney and a little puff of sparkles (at the start of a round they pop up one after another). Fly close (or walk up on the roof) = rescued: they spring up out of the chimney and cheer with arms in a V, a "THANK YOU!" bubble floats up, hearts and stars burst out, then they pop away in sparkles and someone new pops up somewhere else.
  - A yellow arrow above the hero points to the nearest person who needs help.
  - HUD: "RESCUED: N" top-left, 60-second timer top-right. When time runs out: "The sun is up! You rescued N people!" and a PLAY AGAIN button. No way to lose.
  - **One night, sunset to sunrise:** each round starts at sunset (orange-pink sky, a big glowing yellow sun sinking behind the mountains in front of her). In about 8 seconds it fades to the purple night with stars; the moon slowly arcs across the sky. In the last 10 seconds the sky turns pink-orange on the opposite side and the sun rises over the mountains as time runs out. Fog and lights follow the sky; windows keep glowing. The title screen stays night.
  - **Land and walk:** hold S at her lowest flying height and she floats gently down and lands on whatever is under her (street, a roof slope or a chimney top) with a little puff of dust and sparkles. On her feet she stands upright and walks: ⬆️/⬇️ walk forward/back (much slower than flying), ⬅️ ➡️ turn. Her legs step and her arms swing; the cape hangs and sways. Walls and anything taller than a small step stop her (she slides along them); walking off a roof she drops down with a plop. W hops her back into the sky; SPACE web-zips her up to a chimney in front (or just takes off if there is none). The camera comes closer and lower behind her while walking and blends back when she flies. Walking up to someone on a roof rescues them; people on high chimneys are out of reach from the street. The title screen mentions S/W/SPACE for landing.
  - **Rainbow clouds:** 13 puffy low-poly clouds drift slowly with the wind above the town, between a little above her cruise height and just under her highest height (W gets her there). They are tinted by the sky: pink-orange at sunset and dawn, soft lavender at night. She flies right through them; flying into one makes a 7-stripe rainbow pop out of it with sparkles, stars and hearts, which rises a little and fades after about 2 seconds. Each cloud can make a new rainbow again after 5 seconds.
  - **Traffic:** a long chunky coral town bus (white roof stripe, a row of windows, round wheels) and 5 small toy cars (teal, lavender, yellow, cyan, red) drive along the streets between the house lots on the right-hand side, turning at the corners (never U-turns). Headlights and bus windows glow yellow at night and go dim by day, like the house windows; tail lights glow red at night. Scenery only: no crashing, she walks and flies right past them.
  - **Closer camera:** while flying, the camera sits close behind her, a little over her right shoulder (like the concept picture): at rooftop height she fills about half the screen, with the chimneys ahead beside her. It still pulls back and looks down as she flies high. Walking, it is close and low behind her; if a wall, roof, lamp or tree gets in the way it rises over it, and only comes closer if it must. The yellow helper arrow now floats just in front of her.
  - **Shadows from the sun and moon:** one light follows the low sun at sunset and sunrise (warm, long shadows) and the moon at night (bluish, fainter). Houses, roofs, chimneys, trees, lamps, crates, the hero (cape, arms and legs too), the townsfolk, the bus and cars and the clouds cast shadows on roofs and streets. The sharp shadow area follows her round town.
  - **Hero size re-tune** (she is about one-and-a-bit doors tall now): she flies a little closer over roofs and chimneys, web-zips to just above the chimney rim, walks a bit slower with quicker little steps, and bumps into lamp posts, tree trunks and crates as well as walls instead of walking through them.
  - **Sunrise party:** when the 60 seconds are up and the sun rises, she stops and hovers proudly, turning to face the crowd, and the camera pulls back and looks up. Everyone she rescued this round (up to 12) pops up out of the chimneys in front of her and cheers with BOTH arms up and happy jumps, shouting "HOORAY!", "YAY!", "WOO-HOO!", with little hearts and a shower of palette-coloured confetti. People still waiting for help pop away in sparkles. Fireworks whoosh up from the rooftops with sparkly tails and burst into round balls of low-poly sparks (yellow, coral, cyan, lavender, teal, white) that drift down and twinkle out: quick volleys first, then calmer ones. After about 4.5 seconds the end screen shows, and the fireworks and cheering carry on behind it. With 0 rescues there are still fireworks, a "The sun is up! Let's try again!" bubble, and the end screen says "Try again, you can do it!". PLAY AGAIN clears it all.
  - **Background scenery:** outside the town, a green meadow with about 25 rolling low-poly hills topped with clusters of cone pine trees; a river winding along the far side of town with shimmering blue water and a humped, arched stone bridge across it; a church on a hilltop with a pointy square tower, a round tower and a turret, all with glowing windows at night; a windmill with turning sails on a hill, a tall round stone tower with a spiral of windows, and a row of tall town houses along the river bank. Scenery only: it stays outside where she can fly and below the sunset/sunrise sun. Code: `src/scenery.js`.
  - **Flying poses (like `assets/concepts/hero-flying-poses.png`):** her whole body now moves with how she flies, blending smoothly from one pose to the next. **Cruising** (normal forward flight): lying flat, right fist forward, left arm along her side, legs together, cape streaming back. **Zooming** (holding ⬆️ at full speed, and while web-zipping): fully stretched, both fists forward, legs straight, cape flat and long. **Climbing** (W): body tipped steeply up, right fist punching up, cape hanging behind. **Diving** (S): head first down, both arms forward, legs up behind, cape flapping up. **Turning** (⬅️ ➡️): she leans into the turn, the outer arm reaches out for balance, the knees come up a little and the cape swings out. **Hovering** (slowing down or backing up with ⬇️, landing, and the sunrise party): upright, arms a little out, legs dangling and gently swaying, cape waving softly. She bobs and breathes a little all the time. The web still shoots from her right glove; walking is unchanged.
  - **Sculpted houses (House variety):** the town's ~100 houses are now three sculpted 3D models made from the Team's pictures, mixed around town so two neighbours are never the same: the one-storey house with the tall lavender chimney and brick knobs (`assets/models/house2.glb`), the two-storey house (`house3.glb`) and the porch cottage (`house4.glb`). They share one set of colours: peach walls, red roofs, lavender chimneys, golden windows (glowing at night, dim by day), red-brown doors and orange window frames. Each house is turned a quarter turn so a front or a side faces the street, with a small size wobble. Townsfolk stand waist-deep in the tall lavender chimneys (the little red cap block on top is taken off so they fit, with a dark opening inside). Landing, walking, flying over roofs, bumping into chimneys and rescues all follow the real roof, porch and chimney shapes. If the models can't load, simple-shape houses of the same size and with the same chimney spots stand in. Code: `src/town.js`.
  - **Mixed house colours and cozier streets (the Team's wish "mix some colours"):** each house keeps its model but gets one of four soft wall colours, peach, cream, light blue or mint, never the same as the house to its left or behind it. Window frames are wood brown now (not orange). At night every glowing window has a soft amber halo on the wall around it, and the streetlamp light pools are peach; both fade out by day. Streets are a dusty mauve, lawns a muted sage green, and each lawn is edged with a row of staggered flat paving stones in two soft mauves, low-key so windows and people needing help still stand out. Code: `src/town.js`.
